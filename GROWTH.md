### Programmatic pages
- **Brand pages** (~20): specs table, filters, and best pick per level. These are the biggest win.
- **Brand × level / brand × shape** pages, e.g. "Best Nox racket for beginners".
- **Price-tier pages**: under £50/£100/£150/£200, plus €/$ versions.
- **Attribute pages**: shape, weight band, core (soft/hard EVA, foam), carbon/fibreglass, women's, junior.
- **vs pages**: generate them only for the ~100 most-searched pairs (same brand line, adjacent years such as Vertex 03 vs 04). Pages for all 50k pairs would be thin content and risk an index bloat penalty, so noindex the rest.
- **Year pages**: "[model] 2025 vs 2026".
- Each page needs unique text generated from the specs, plus FAQ schema, Product/ItemList schema and breadcrumbs.

### Apply to first
